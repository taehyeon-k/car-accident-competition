## Local baseline identified

- Run: `runs/stage3_v2/V3_tcnssm_100ep`, best EMA checkpoint `best.pt`, seed 42, selected at epoch 91 of 100. Its saved config and metrics are the reproducibility record. The submission used steering 5 degrees and acceleration ±0.5 m/s² and scored 0.7388 on the real leaderboard (`HANDOFF.md`). The saved training config and offline metrics use acceleration ±0.25 m/s², so leaderboard and offline numbers have different decoding thresholds.
- Data: 41 complete BATON-Sample route folders containing `qcamera.mp4` and `vehicle_dynamics.csv`; the local adapter has **no metadata-based quality filter** beyond requiring both files. It loads `time_s`, `vEgo`, `aEgo`, `steeringAngleDeg`; removes invalid/duplicate timestamps and sorts time. Source selection of the upstream sample is not recorded in this repository.
- Split: `manifests_raw/train.jsonl` has 1,693 thirty-second segments from 33 route folders (14.024 h). `manifests_raw/val.jsonl` has 466 segments from 8 route folders (3.856 h). The split is by route folder ID using seed 42. It is not fully route-family disjoint: `route_24_1` and `route_25_1` are in validation while sibling recordings occur in training. This preexisting split is unchanged.
- Cache: PyAV PTS decode, nearest frame on exact 10 Hz grid (max 0.04 s selection error), resize to 192×336, SEA-RAFT-S motion and geometry cache, 30 s segments. CAN is interpolated at selected **actual video times**, with a 0.25 s maximum signal gap. Direct `aEgo` is smoothed at 0.5 and 1.5 s; speed derivatives are auxiliary. Stopped is `vEgo <= 0.15 m/s`. Steering is steering-wheel degrees, positive left. Cache schema 2 holds 10×96×168 motion channels, 20 physics channels, actual/target times, validity, and clipped raw signals.
- Training: TCN+SSM, 96-frame crop, event sampling 0.5, horizontal flip 0.5, batch 8, AdamW 3e-4, weight decay 0.05, cosine schedule with 5% warmup, bf16, EMA 0.999, 100 epochs, no early stopping. The saved run recorded peak allocated VRAM 5,825 MiB. The exact loss weights and remaining settings are in the run's `config.yaml`.
- Offline baseline at the saved ±0.25 decoder: train full-clip acceleration Macro-F1 0.7284, steering Macro-F1 0.9052, score 0.7814; validation acceleration 0.7522, steering 0.8962, score 0.7954. Validation class F1: acceleration 0.6869 / 0.6694 / 0.6944 / 0.9581 (accelerating/decelerating/constant/stopped), steering 0.8592 / 0.9683 / 0.8612 (left/straight/right). Steering evaluation masks ground-truth STOPPED frames. Confusion matrices are in the run's `metrics.json`.
- The saved config and `manifests_raw` refer to `/workspace/cache/stage3/motion_raw`, which is absent here. Equivalent compressed caches exist in `/workspace/cache/stage3/motion`; this must be remapped in a separate manifest to rerun baseline evaluation without altering the original manifests.

## DriveDNA metadata acquired without video download

`python -m stage3.scripts.external_data_inventory --source drivedna --output /workspace/data/stage3/drivedna/metadata` recorded the official repository revision `c8cb05426abdc332749cee89d333680223142c2a`, 63 paired CSV/MP4 drives, 6,548,162,255 video bytes and 552,417,519 telemetry bytes. The dataset card describes 60 drives with 27 columns at 10 Hz, including `time_s`, `vEgo`, `aEgo`, `steeringAngleDeg`, and `is_human`; three legacy drives have only `t`, `yaw_ned`, and `vEgo` at 20 Hz and cannot meet the direct-acceleration/steering training contract. It also describes video and CSV beginning at drive time zero, with differing end times. The file contents have since been inspected and filtered for the corrected experiment. See [the official dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HenryYHW/DriveDNA-Sample).

The BATON procedure provides no numeric or event metadata rejection criteria to translate to DriveDNA. The corresponding initial DriveDNA eligibility check is video/CSV pairing plus availability of the four canonical telemetry fields; further signal quality checks must use the actual CSV data before selecting videos. The dataset card's `is_human` mask is a possible domain distinction, but filtering it would introduce a new criterion absent from the baseline and cannot be assumed without deciding the experiment's population.
