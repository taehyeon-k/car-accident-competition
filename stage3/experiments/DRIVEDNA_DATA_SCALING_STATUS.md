## Cache-loading performance fix

The synchronous loader left epoch 1 unfinished after roughly 50 minutes. A two-file benchmark found that `torch.load` on the gzip stream repeatedly seeks and decompresses: BATON took 0.774 s and DriveDNA 0.957 s. Decompressing once into a seekable memory buffer took 0.145 s and 0.166 s including torch parsing; motion tensors were identical. This is a 5.3–5.8x improvement in the measured loading component, not a measured end-to-end training speedup.

The loader now decompresses once. Training uses two loader workers and `prefetch_factor=1`, with the same batch size and training recipe. Progress is printed for the first batch and every 20 batches. Thirteen relevant tests passed, including compressed/uncompressed tensor-layout roundtrips and checkpoint-selection behavior. The unfinished first epoch was restarted to load the fix; no checkpoint or scored epoch was discarded. GPU allocation remains capped at fraction 0.55. Whole-epoch throughput remains to be measured.
